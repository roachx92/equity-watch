## Q2 FY2026 — reported 2026-07-30

> **No Tripwire fired. But Tripwire #1's funding leg is now fully satisfied and only its duration leg is outstanding — the closest this file has come to a fired trigger.** The quarter's $8.22B net loss is 101% a non-cash bitcoin mark and analytically near-empty; the four things that actually happened were: **(1)** the deferred-tax shield was exhausted via a full valuation allowance, so a $8.22B pre-tax loss carried a **$1.6M tax expense**; **(2)** the preferred ATM shut completely — **$0** of STRC/STRK/STRF/STRD/STRE issuance in Q3 QTD against **$5.465B** of STRC in Q2 — leaving the common ATM (**$1,276.0M**, Jul 1–26, at ~0.65x gross BTC NAV) as the sole funding valve; **(3)** the company's own per-share scorecard went backwards, YTD BTC Yield **9.4% → 4.5%**; and **(4)** management de-levered pre-emptively and cheaply — convert principal cut **18%** to $6.71B, $25.0M of STRC retired at 13.5% below par, the USD Reserve built to $3.75B. The Edge's two halves were both corroborated in the same print: the mechanism inverted *and* the release valves worked. **Thesis unchanged in direction; the clock on it materially shorter.**

**OCF vs. net income — and why the standard framing has to be adapted here.** The §I.3(b) test asks whether reported revenue is turning into cash. For MSTR the answer is *yes and it does not matter*: the software business converts roughly break-even on a ~$490M annualized revenue base, and in the last quarter with an observable OCF (Q1'26) the divergence from net income was **100.0%** the bitcoin mark plus the $1.92B deferred-tax release. **The economically correct version of the question is: does anything the company owns generate the fixed charges it owes? It does not.** The annual preferred-dividend-plus-interest bill is **~$1.76B derived** (~$1,729M preferred + ~$34.6M convert cash coupons) — cross-checked against the company's own arithmetic of $3.75B ÷ 2.1 years = **$1.786B** — against a software operating loss of −$15.6M. **100% of that bill is funded from issuance or asset sales.** That is not a working-capital warning; it is the instrument's design, and the correct thing to monitor is the funding valve, not DSO.

**⭐ The one genuine warning in this block, and it is a trend a level would have hidden.** The USD Reserve grew from **$2.25B on 2026-02-01** to **$3.75B on 2026-07-26**, **+67%**. Over the same stretch the coverage it buys fell from **"approximately 2.5 years"** to **"over 2.1 years."** The reserve grew two-thirds and the runway *shortened*, because the annual fixed charge roughly **doubled from ~$900M to ~$1.79B in under six months** — driven by STRC's $7.53B of YTD issuance at 11–12%. That is the negative-carry mechanism the Edge names, measured from the company's own two disclosures, and it is the single most useful number this quarter produced. **Reconciling it against the deck's narrative, as §I.3(b) requires:** management presented $3.75B and 2.1 years as evidence of strengthening liquidity. Both facts are true; the derivative is the one that isn't in the narrative.

**Runway against the pre-commitment:** **25.2 months** versus Tripwire #2's ~6-month floor — **4.2x the threshold**, and 2.1x the Board's own 12-month policy minimum. Not close.

### 2. Assessment — what the call does to the thesis

#### (a) The underlying investment thesis

**In one line:** MSTR is a levered, fixed-charge claim on bitcoin whose defining premium has inverted into a discount, and the question is *durability* — whether that discount self-perpetuates or whether management's release valves prevent it becoming permanent. **What has to be true:** (i) the discount is not self-reinforcing; (ii) the ~$1.79B/yr preferred charge can be funded without permanently ratcheting BTC-per-share down; (iii) BTC does not break structurally below the cost basis; (iv) no forced-liquidation mechanism exists.

- **Leg (ii) — WEAKENED, and this is the print's main event.** The company's own YTD scorecard went **BTC Yield 9.4% (2026-05-03) → 4.5% (2026-07-26)**, BTC $ Gain **$4.97B → $1.95B** — roughly **33,400 BTC of year-to-date per-share gain given back in under three months**, in exactly the window when the common ATM was the sole funding valve. That is not commentary; it is the ratchet, in the company's own furnished metric. Note what management quoted instead: Le cited "+5% Bitcoin Per Share" (a *quarter* figure) and Kang cited "203,683 sats, +6% year-over-year" (an *annual* figure). **Neither executive quoted the YTD series that reversed**, which appears only in the un-narrated summary bullets.
- **Leg (i) — SUPPORTED, unexpectedly.** The release valves are not theoretical: convert principal cut **18% ($8.21B → $6.71B)** by buying $1.5B of the 0% 2029s at ~8% below par, **$25.0M of STRC retired at 13.5% below par**, the reserve built to 25 months, 18 months of unbroken dividends through a deep drawdown, and **zero deferrals.** Crucially this is de-levering **pre-emptively**, before any trigger — the opposite of the distressed sequencing the bear case assumes.
- **Leg (iii) — UNTESTED-to-slightly-worse.** BTC marked at **$58,558.86 on 2026-06-30**, the cycle's lowest quarter-end and essentially at the prior ~$58K low; it has since recovered to **$64,725.31** (2026-07-30 settled). The quarter-end mark is what produced the $8.3B loss and consumed the tax shield.
- **Leg (iv) — SUPPORTED and materially strengthened.** There is **no exercisable convert event in 2026 at all.** The earliest holder put is **2027-09-15** (2028 notes, $183.19 conversion price); the only 2026 date is 2026-12-04, a *company* soft call on the 2029s requiring MSTR ≥ **$874.12** — unreachable by ~9x. **Discounting forward numbers by the Guidance track record:** the table's instruction is to discount management's forwards — and the finding is that **there are none left to discount** on the growth side. - **Fundamental — NEGATIVE, and one item is permanent.** Tax-shield exhaustion is a genuine, irreversible removal of an offset: every further BTC decline is now unshielded GAAP loss. The fixed charge roughly doubling to ~$1.79B while coverage fell is fundamental. Per-share accretion reversing is the fundamental driver of the entire equity story, and it reversed.

The capital stack got measurably safer. The offsetting structural negative is severe though: **the preferred ATM is shut** — $0 across all five series in Q3 QTD against $5.465B of STRC in Q2 — because management will not issue below par. - **De-escalated — refinancing/liquidity.** No convert put until 2027-09-15, principal cut 18%, 25 months of dividend coverage, no covenants. This is the clearest risk improvement in the file.

### 2. Assessment — what the call does to the thesis

#### (a) The underlying investment thesis

The deep-dive's conjunction (2026-07-21 §15): *BTC rallies above the ~$75.5K cost basis **and** the mNAV premium re-establishes above ~1.0x (ideally ~1.22x) **before** fixed charges force enough BTC sales to permanently shrink Bitcoin-per-share — all three, in that order.*
