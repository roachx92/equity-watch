### 2. Assessment — what the call does to the thesis

#### (a) The underlying investment thesis

The deep-dive's conjunction (2026-07-21 §15): *BTC rallies above the ~$75.5K cost basis **and** the mNAV premium re-establishes above ~1.0x (ideally ~1.22x) **before** fixed charges force enough BTC sales to permanently shrink Bitcoin-per-share — all three, in that order.*

- **Leg 1 — BTC above cost basis: weakened, partly by management's own hand.** Q1 is the quarter the polarity flipped (−$6.04B from +$8.42B). The mark is the market's doing; the cost basis *rising* $74,997 → $75,694 is management's — $7.25B deployed at ~19.4% above the quarter-end price.
- **Leg 2 — the premium above 1.0x/1.22x: supported at the print, falsified eight weeks later.** Q1 is the **last quarter on file with the accretion engine operational** (1.27x vs their own 1.22x). Everything since is the other side of that line.
- **Leg 3 — before fixed charges force the substitution: weakened, and now filed.** $220.3M of ATM proceeds diverted to carry (12.7x YoY); BPS accretion +11.0% → +3.2%, which the company attributes to a lower premium; fixed charges ~$596M/yr (Q3'25) → ~$1,086M (Q1'26) → ~$1,495–1,533M today. **The required sequence is running backwards.**
- **Discounted by the track record.** Management's *payment* commitments are 100% honoured and can be taken at face value; its *metric* commitments have been missed or redefined every time. Leg 2 rested entirely on a management metric. It is the leg that broke.

**Net: unchanged in framing, weakened in position.** Q1 falsified nothing — it supplied the baseline against which the June–July collapse is measured, and it filed the mechanism.

#### (b) Re-rating drivers (four types, kept separate)

- **Fundamental — negative.** No discount removed. BPS accretion down two-thirds; $220.3M diverted from BTC to carry; cost basis raised into a falling market. The "forced sales compress the premium" driver went from hypothetical to disclosed.
