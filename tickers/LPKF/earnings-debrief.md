### 2. Assessment — what the call does to the thesis

#### (a) The underlying investment thesis

The re-underwritten (2026-07-19) conjunction: *(a) glass-core industrializes ~2027–2029; (b) LIDE converts pilots into series orders before the balance sheet forces a raise; (c) LPKF holds the first HVM reference socket against Philoptics/Han's; (d) any raise is not executed at a de-rated price into the 89.4% float.*

- **Leg (a) — industrialization clock:** *supported.* GlaSSEM 2H27, Absolics near AMD qualification, BOE's strategic commitment, TSMC 2H28 — plus a glass *maker* buying a LIDE qualification tool, which is the supply chain physically assembling itself. The TAM upsize is directionally corroborated.
- **Leg (b) — orders before the raise:** *weakened on both sides of the race.* The order side slipped again ("this fiscal year"); the balance-sheet side deteriorated faster (net debt, ~2–3 quarters of headroom). The two clocks this leg races against each other both moved the wrong way.
- **Leg (c) — the first HVM socket:** *untested, slightly pressured.* No rival won a named first HVM socket; Chinese TGV production orders (22 Jul) are a lower tier — but LPKF also stopped publishing its competitive map the same print.
- **Leg (d) — raise terms:** *untested, conditions worsened.* No raise, no adverse facility change — but every quarter of burn moves a hypothetical raise toward a weaker price, and spot is ~51% off the high.

**Net: thesis corroborated in framing, weakened in position.** The print validated *what the thesis says matters* (financing duration, the downstream gate) while making the company's situation inside that framing worse. The Guidance track record now discounts three separate unresolved management commitments.

#### (b) Re-rating drivers (four types, kept separate)

- **Fundamental:** **negative.** No discount-removing event (no series order); the funder deterioration *added* a balance-sheet discount. The reaffirmed guide, if somehow hit, would be a large H2 positive — but it earns no credit in advance against this record.
- **Structural:** **improved at the margin.** The €1.7bn TAM (direction corroborated), the roadmap expansion (CPO on glass, glass bonding), the glass-maker qualification order, and the intact-to-forward industry clock all raise the option's eventual prize. Montega's €22 is this bucket being priced by one broker.
- **Sentiment/technical:** **the split emerged.** First target above spot (Montega €22) reopens the sell-side re-rate channel the deep-dive called closed; simultaneously the price made a post-print low. Targets straddling spot means the name is now a genuine disagreement, not a consensus fade.
- **Macro:** not a driver this print (tape was flat).

**A re-rate is multiple expansion — and this print produced a genuine, visible divergence:** the option's ceiling was marked *up* (TAM, Montega) while the equity's price and funding floor were marked *down*. The bucket the print weighted most is **fundamental (negative, balance-sheet-driven)**; the structural improvement is real but pays on a 2028–2030 clock the balance sheet may not reach unassisted.

#### (c) Risks

- **Funding/dilution — ESCALATED, now the dominant risk.** €4.0m cash, −€5.7m net debt, equity ratio 62.8% (−10.4pts in six months), ~€16.5m total liquidity vs ~€5.75m/qtr average H1 burn. The deep-dive's "financed for quarters, thesis needs years" mismatch got one print worse.
- **Funder erosion — ESCALATED and broadened.** Solar −80% *and* Welding −37% with a negative EBIT swing: two of the three cash-generative segments are now loss-making. The option's carry cost is rising as the carrier weakens.
- **Guidance credibility — escalated.** FY2026 reaffirmed with H1 at ~35% of the low end and 3× through the margin floor. A cut at 9M or FY would be the *third* strike in the table.
- **Rival/dual-sourcing — unchanged-to-amber.** Chinese TGV commercial orders at panel level; no named HVM displacement. The dropped competitor map is a soft negative on visibility, not evidence of a loss.
- **The gating risk (downstream qualification) — unchanged, and now self-evidencing:** the quarter's own order mix (qualification tools, not production tools) is the gate made visible.

**Pre-Mortem update.** The named mechanism — *industrialization slips right, the base business can't fund the wait, a de-rated raise into the thin float, the reference line goes to a rival* — moved **closer on the funding arm** (net debt crossed, headroom ~2–3 quarters) and **no closer on the rival arm** (no named socket loss). The funding arm is now unambiguously the near arm, exactly as the 2026-07-19 report predicted ("the tripwire most likely to fire first, because the burn is arithmetic").
