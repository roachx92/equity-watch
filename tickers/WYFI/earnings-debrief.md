### 2. Assessment — what the call does to the thesis

#### (a) The underlying investment thesis

The deep-dive's conjunction: **(a)** Nscale bills at margin and the Q2 print confirms it; **(b)** NC-1 permanent financing closes on non-punitive terms; **(c)** a second, non-Nscale tenant signs the next tranche; **(d)** parent support holds without forcing a dilutive raise.

- **(a) — WEAKENED.** The leg the print existed to settle, settled against: zero Nscale revenue, colocation down sequentially, a fourth slipped date. The offset is real — billing has commenced, run-rate guided to 2026-08-31 — but **that forward number is discounted by a 0-for-5 milestone record**: same management, same asset, fifth date.
- **(b) — MODESTLY SUPPORTED, for the first time.** Consortium exclusivity is the first hard progress since May and RBC was **upsized**, not impaired. Against it: no size, rate or close date, a fresh no-assurance caveat, and a bridge **$80m drawn at 50.6%**. The leg moved forward and got more expensive at once.
- **(c) — UNTESTED; the distance did not narrow.** No second tenant, marketing **not started**, and **Nscale holds priority notification rights on the second tranche**.
- **(d) — SUPPORTED.** Bit Digital **"does not intend to sell WhiteFiber shares in 2026,"** and funded the facility off its ETH treasury rather than selling stock. No margin call, no WhiteFiber raise, ATM or shelf.

**One leg weakened decisively, one improved at a price, one untested, one held.** The thesis is not broken; its fastest-resolving leg failed on schedule.

#### (b) Re-rating drivers

- **Fundamental — moved, but not as it looks.** The deep-dive's largest driver was *"Q2 confirms Nscale billing at margin."* **It did not.** What re-rated the stock was a beat built on a non-recurring fee plus $540m of new *cloud* contracts — a different, smaller business than the colocation backlog the thesis underwrites. **A discount was removed that the thesis never argued existed, while the one it does argue about is intact.**
- **Structural — unchanged, still adverse.** Still 40 MW against a cohort transacting in 300 MW+ blocks. Nscale's IPO prep (~$25bn talk vs. a $14.6bn March post, ~$51bn contracted revenue, ~10 GW target) makes the tenant larger and more independent. **Timing note:** an Nscale S-1 on a ~4–8 week horizon would put audited financials, risk factors and plausibly the WhiteFiber lease into a public filing — **the first dated, checkable read Tripwire #2 has ever had.**
