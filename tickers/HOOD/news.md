## Thesis context (one-paragraph)
Robinhood is a self-clearing US retail broker that has added options, crypto (incl. **Bitstamp**, acquired 2025-06-02), retirement, the **Gold** subscription (4.84M subscribers, 17% attach), a credit card and banking (via Coastal Community Bank), international (>1M funded customers; WonderFi closed 2026-06-01) and — since 2025 — **event contracts** (prediction markets, mostly sports), partly through **Rothera**, a CFTC-registered DCM/DCO/SEF JV with Susquehanna that it consolidates but does not operationally control. **Q2 2026 revenue was $1,308M (+32% YoY)**: options $342M, **event contracts $156M (from $10M)**, equities $129M, crypto $100M (−38%), net interest $389M (+9%), other $143M (10-Q Note 6). Event contracts supplied **~46% of Q2's and ~72% of Q1's** YoY revenue growth (derived); excluding them revenue grew +18% (Q2) and +4% (Q1), and excluding both event contracts and crypto **+28% / +23%**. Profitability is high (Q2 adj. EBITDA $741M, 57%) but **Q2 GAAP net income ($561M per XBRL; $573M per the release) includes ~$129M of gains, mostly the RVI deconsolidation**. **Concentration: Citadel Securities = 16% of total net revenue; market makers and exchanges = 47%**; in Q2 Rule 606 data Citadel took 41.9% of PFOF and Citadel + IMC ~78% of options PFOF. Balance sheet: ~$5.36B cash, $3.9B RHS net capital, $0 drawn on $4.25B of revolvers (the $3.25B RHS line is **364-day, renewing ~March 2027**), a **$2.2B 0% convert due 2029-10-01** (conversion ~$174.42, capped calls to ~$237.85), a $21.6B margin book (+127%) and $4.67B of customer securities re-pledged to clearing houses. **The event-contract line rests on CEA preemption of state gaming law** — the Third Circuit for Kalshi (April 2026), the Ninth (2026-08-28) and Sixth against, **New Jersey's cert petition filed ~2026-09-02**; HOOD has already stopped new sports contracts in Nevada (2025-12-01) and lost a preliminary injunction in Michigan. Founders control ~58% of votes on ~12% of economics and are selling (Tenev ~$75M Sept–Oct 2026). Settled close **$112.00 (2026-10-06)**, ~$100.7B equity value, **~49x trailing GAAP earnings**, 27% below the $152.46 closing high (2025-10-09) and 72% above the $65.16 low (2026-03-30). Next print: **Q3 2026 on 2026-10-27** after the close. The debate is not quality — it is what the growth is made of, and whether a court lets it stay.

## Edge (variant view — what the crowd may have wrong)
*Seeded **2026-10-08** from [`reports/2026-10-08.md`](reports/2026-10-08.md) §18, verbatim. First coverage — nothing superseded. Because there was no pre-committed Edge to deny, the mandatory §L adversarial pass was aimed at the **drafted** Edge with the fact-check duty over every load-bearing figure; it found a real problem — the draft mis-described the consensus and treated the legal risk as a single Supreme Court binary — and the Edge below is the re-derived version. Reconciliation in the report's closing block.*

> The consensus already knows event contracts are HOOD's growth leg — it is the bull case. Morgan Stanley upgraded to Overweight on 2026-09-01 explicitly on prediction markets (~23% revenue CAGR to ~$8.0B by 2028), BofA moved to $156 on 2026-10-05, and Bernstein models ~$586M of FY2026 prediction-market revenue. The line went from $10M to $156M a quarter in a year and supplied **~46% of Q2'26 and ~72% of Q1'26 year-on-year revenue growth**. Where the consensus and the tape agree is on *how* the legal risk to that line will arrive: as **a single Supreme Court binary** — two cert petitions are pending (New Jersey's, No. 26-299, and HOOD's own, No. 26-338) — to be priced when the Court acts.
>
> **The differentiated view is that the legal risk is not a future binary; it is an attrition already running, and the market is not pricing it as it happens.** HOOD has stopped new sports contracts in **Nevada (2025-12-01)** and **Michigan (2026-09-09)**; **Ohio and Tennessee** became enforceable after the Sixth Circuit (2026-09-25); **Washington**'s standstill ends with the Ninth Circuit en banc process; a Connecticut halt order has been reported. Each exit removes a slice of the line without a single repricing day — the stock moved **−5.0%, −1.8% and −1.2%** on the Ninth Circuit ruling, the Michigan halt and the Sixth Circuit ruling, against **+16.6% in one session** on crypto beta. And resolution is slow and multi-path — the courts, CFTC rulemaking, or Congress — with a merits ruling needing a grant by roughly January 2027 to land by June 2027 and otherwise slipping to 2028. **So the base case for the next 12–24 months is attrition, not resolution**, and a model that compounds the line at the consensus growth rate is compounding a shrinking map. One more trap: event contracts' *share of growth* will fall mechanically from Q3'26 because the line was only broken out from Q1'26 and the Q3/Q4'25 base is buried in "other transaction" — the concentration will look smaller in the headline exactly as the attrition bites. **The variable to underwrite is the *level* of event-contract revenue against a shrinking set of open states, and the non-event, non-crypto core's growth through the next retail downturn — not the product count and not the share-of-growth statistic.**
>
> **What this Edge explicitly does not claim.** It does not claim event contracts will be banned — a pro-preemption resolution would make them federally protected and compounding, with Rothera capturing exchange economics; that is the bull case, and Tripwire #5 is written to catch it. It does not claim the whole $156M is at risk — crypto-linked and non-sports contracts sit outside the legal dispute, and the CEO says crypto-linked contracts are an "outsized slice"; the sports share is **undisclosed**, so the revenue actually exposed is lower by an unknown amount. It does not claim the core is weak — revenue excluding event contracts and crypto grew **+23% (Q1) and +28% (Q2)**. And it does not claim fraud, aggressive accounting or solvency risk.

## Tripwires (pre-committed exit / re-underwrite triggers)
*Seeded 2026-10-08 from [`reports/2026-10-08.md`](reports/2026-10-08.md) §18 — verbatim, split one bullet per trigger (a formatting change only). Trigger identity (the numbering) originates here. Expiry dates in the table below (§J.4).*

Pre-commit to re-underwriting or exiting if ANY of these fire:

- **(1)** **Sports event contracts are halted — by HOOD, a court order or a stipulation — in a fifth state** (Nevada and Michigan are the first two as of 2026-10-08; a third and fourth are tolerated as the attrition the Edge expects), **or the Supreme Court denies both pending cert petitions (No. 26-299 and No. 26-338) while the Ninth or Sixth Circuit rulings stand, or rules against CEA preemption on the merits.** **Committed action: re-underwrite downward** — attrition has outrun the line's growth, or the legal question has closed against it.
- **(2)** **Event-contract revenue (10-Q revenue-disaggregation note) declines quarter on quarter for two consecutive quarters.** **Committed action: re-underwrite** — the line is shrinking in absolute terms, not just losing share of growth; this is the level-based test the Edge says matters.
- **(3)** **Revenue excluding both event contracts and crypto grows less than 15% year on year for two consecutive quarters**, computed from the recast prior-year comparatives in the current 10-Q revenue note (1H'26 baseline: +23% Q1, +28% Q2). **Committed action: re-underwrite** — the core is decelerating beneath the new line and §14's "defensible without event contracts" leg fails.
- **(4)** **Reported annualized net-deposit growth falls below 10% for three consecutive months** in Robinhood's monthly operating data (Aug 2026: 14%; Jun–Aug monthly $10.1B → $5.6B → $4.0B). The series is flattered by Trump Account contributions (from July 2026) and acquisitions (TradePMR, WonderFi), so the organic rate is already lower than reported. **Committed action: re-underwrite** — asset gathering is the leading indicator of every durable revenue line.
- **(5) [UPWARD — re-underwrite, do not exit]** **The Supreme Court rules that the CEA pre-empts state law for exchange-listed sports event contracts, or Congress enacts exclusive federal jurisdiction over them.** **Committed action: re-underwrite upward** — the attrition branch of the Edge is falsified on its own terms. *(Recorded as a numbered trigger deliberately: a falsifier that is never written down is never checked.)*

Any one = the thesis is impaired — or, for #5, vindicated; decide the action now, not after the drawdown.
