**Reconciling against the deck's own narrative — two contradictions worth naming.**

1. **The DSO jump is unattributable from the filing.** The 10-Q carries **no receivable-concentration disclosure and names no counterparty share of AR**. The benign explanation, which I think is the likely one, is that the AR and the new $25.5M deferred revenue are two sides of the same HPC change-order gross-up — billed-but-uncollected on one side, collected-but-unearned on the other. **That is my inference; the filing does not say it, and there is nothing in the filing to check it against.** Given a Tripwire watches tenant credit, the absence of a concentration line on the exact quarter receivables went from 1.5 to 73.6 days is a disclosure gap, not a reassurance.
2. **"Rent has commenced" and "cash interest has commenced" landed in the same quarter, and only one of them is in the highlights.** The release leads with Black Pearl rent commencing (August, post-quarter). It does not mention that Q2 was the first quarter Cipher paid cash interest, or that OCF swung $335M negative sequentially. Both facts are in the filed statements; only one is in the bullets.

**The honest counterweight.** Most of the $645.8M of AP/accrued is project-level construction payable out of the **$3.728B of restricted project escrow that is already funded for exactly that purpose** — so this is not a liquidity squeeze, and reading it as one would be wrong. The genuine finding is narrower and still real: the **cash-capex line materially understates the build run-rate**, and any statement about corporate liquidity has to net unpaid construction obligations against the **$832M** unrestricted figure, not the $4.56B headline.

**Language shift versus Q1 — primary-sourced, and the strongest single finding in this section.** The Q1 release (2026-05-05) carried the banner *"Development Timeline **on Track** at Barber Lake and Black Pearl Data Centers"* and, in the bullets, *"**The project remains on schedule**"* — once for each site. **Neither the banner nor either sentence appears in the Q2 release.** Both sites now read *"continues to progress towards completion."* And the tell is what survived: the same Q2 release says Stingray *"construction continues to progress **on schedule**."* **Management kept the schedule affirmation for Stingray and dropped it for Barber Lake and Black Pearl in the same document.** For Black Pearl the omission is almost certainly benign — the site *beat* schedule, so "on schedule" became the wrong word. **Barber Lake is the one that matters**, and its written schedule guarantee was withdrawn while it is still pre-delivery, eight weeks from the quarter's single most important cash-flow event. The call reaffirmed September delivery and October rent verbally, so this is **not a disclosed slip** — but the asymmetry between the written and the spoken is exactly what to carry into Q3.

**What was dodged.** ERCOT Batch Zero timing was asked twice (Byrd/Morgan Stanley, Petersen/Jefferies) and deflected both times as *"too fresh"* and *"hard to predict"* — **no base case, no upside case, on the single most material near-term regulatory binary in the story.** Capex per MW was asked twice by two different firms and twice declined. Reveille/Ulysses tenant identities were withheld pending closure (defensible).

**And the most important thing about this Q&A is what was never asked.** Across eight analysts and ~16 questions: **nobody asked about Fluidstack's creditworthiness, nobody asked about its unclosed funding round, nobody asked about the Google backstop, nobody asked about the penny-warrant Top-Up ratchet, and nobody asked about the $150.5M warrant remeasurement that was 56% of the quarter's net loss.** That is a sell-side coverage gap, not a management dodge — management cannot deflect a question not asked. The practical consequence is identical: **the structurally riskiest part of this capital structure went entirely unexamined on the record.** It is also, precisely, the Edge.

**On the print day the sector tape rose and CIFR fell 15.65%. Essentially the whole move is company-specific** — this is not a sector datapoint being misfiled as a company one, it is the reverse of the July pattern.

**But the base matters, and this is the part the wire recaps missed.** CIFR closed **2026-07-29 at $17.69**, then ripped **+28.07% on 7/30** — the session the entire cohort ripped as Citadel absorbed Situational Awareness's entire public book in one pre-open block, removing a forced seller — and added **+8.24% on 8/03** to enter the print at $24.16, **up ~36.6% in three sessions on a pure flow event with no CIFR news.** By the 8/06 close of $18.21 it sat **+2.9% above its pre-squeeze 7/29 level. CIFR round-tripped the entire squeeze. Its peers did not** — IREN closed 8/06 still ~29% above its own pre-rebound level. **That asymmetry is the cleanest measure of the company-specific damage there is**, and it says the print destroyed the squeeze premium and nothing more.

**Was it a re-rate, or price tracking earnings?** **A genuine de-rate, and a large one** — because the denominator did not move. Contracted capacity was unchanged at 700 MW, contracted revenue unchanged at ~$11.4B, and average annualized NOI was rebased **up** from ~$787M to ~$793M. Against that:

**What the market reacted to.** A genuine revenue and EPS miss, amplified by the unwind of a three-day flow-driven squeeze, on top of a cohort-wide ERCOT overhang that landed the session *before* the print. **Not primarily a thesis event** — nothing in the print impaired the contracted book, and the largest single line in the loss was a warrant mark that moves *inversely* to the share price from here.

**One reported item I could not verify and am therefore not treating as a driver:** a widely-circulated report of CEO Tyler Page selling 225,000 shares for ~$4.94M around the print. **EDGAR shows no CIFR Form 4 of any kind filed on or after 2026-06-17** (latest: filed 2026-06-16, period 2026-06-14), verified directly against `data.sec.gov`. If the sale occurred on or after 8/04 the Form 4 may simply not be due yet — but **as of today it is unconfirmed at primary source**, and it does not enter this assessment. It is on the watch list in §3.
