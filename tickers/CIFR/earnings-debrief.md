### 2. What the call does to the thesis

#### (a) The underlying investment thesis

The deep-dive's "what has to be true" (2026-09-28 §15): **per-hall delivery lands through Q4'26–Q1'27 without rent credits; capex peaks and OCF inflects before the 2031 wall; ERCOT's spring 2027 allocation preserves the pipeline; and the market resumes paying for contracted backlog.**

- **Per-hall delivery without rent credits — SUPPORTED on Barber Lake, WEAKENED elsewhere.** Barber Lake's per-hall schedule was reaffirmed and the CEO says the first 51 MW went ready-for-service on 11-02 (verbal only). Discount it by the track record: management **beat** Black Pearl's original date, **but the Barber Lake single-date guide was replaced** on 9/24 and the ~9/28 "no new capital" assurance **was contradicted** two weeks later. Meanwhile Black Pearl (18 MW delivered of 300) and Stingray both moved to undated re-phased schedules, and the 10-Q confirms three Black Pearl amendments revising milestone dates. No credit incurred.
- **Capex peaks and OCF inflects — WEAKENED.** Capex 3.8× QoQ, OCF still negative, cost/MW up 25%. The peak is not visible yet.

### 2. What the call does to the thesis

#### (a) The underlying investment thesis

The deep-dive's thesis in one line: *the debate is not whether the $11.4B backlog is real or the counterparties creditworthy — it is **conversion**, and conversion risk is concentrated on a single Sept–Oct 2026 date far more than consensus admits.* Its "what has to be true" is a four-part conjunction. Leg by leg:

- **(a) Barber Lake and Black Pearl energize and commence in the Sept–Oct 2026 window — SUPPORTED, materially, and this is the quarter's strongest fact.** Black Pearl delivered first capacity **at the beginning of August, two months early, and rent has commenced**, under a 2026-07-24 lease amendment executed **at the tenant's request**. Half of the leg is not merely on track; it is done, early, and the AWS parent guarantee on ~$5.5B / 300 MW is now operative rather than contingent. Barber Lake was reaffirmed for September delivery and October rent. Discounted by the Guidance track record, this is the one guide that has come due and it was **beaten**, so the reaffirmation of Barber Lake carries more weight than it did last quarter — though note the *written* schedule affirmation was simultaneously withdrawn (§1(c)).
- **(b) ERCOT classifies all three sites as studied/committed rather than excluded on 8/07 — LEFT UNTESTED, and structurally deferred.** ERCOT market notice **M-A080326-01 of 2026-08-03** states that, on a letter received that day from Governor Abbott directing a verification process, **ERCOT will not notify service providers of any Large Load's Batch Zero classification by August 7, 2026.** It will seek a PUCT good-cause exception ahead of the **2026-08-20** open meeting. **No revised date exists.** This leg cannot now be resolved on the report's own timetable — the question was withdrawn rather than answered.
- **(c) Fluidstack remains able to pay ~$380M/yr — LEFT LARGELY UNTESTED, mildly supported.** The first affirmative evidence in either direction: the tenant *"has commenced beneficial use of the facility, including partial occupancy of the building and deployment of network racks,"* and **$25.5M of change-order cash was collected** — the first HPC-tenant money Cipher has ever received. Against that, the ~$1B / ~$18B round reported 2026-04-14 is **still unconfirmed as closed at ~16 weeks**, one co-lead was deleveraged out of public markets on 7/29–30, and **no analyst raised any of it.**
- **(d) The share price sits above ~$17.79 when the Barber Lake window opens — WEAKENED, and this is where the print did its damage.** The 10-Q reconfirms the Top-Up Threshold at **$430.0M** against 24,178,576 warrant shares — **$17.784/share**, measured on the business day *before* the exercise period begins, i.e. before Barber Lake commencement. The **2026-08-06 settled close of $18.21 sits 2.4% above it**, against 25.5% above at the 7/31 close a week earlier. Roughly eight weeks of headroom, and 2.4% of it.

**Net: the thesis's conversion leg strengthened materially and its dilution leg weakened materially, in the same print.** That is not a wash — they are the two halves of the same date, and the print moved them in opposite directions.

#### (b) Re-rating drivers

*Kept typed per Section D. A re-rate is multiple expansion or contraction, not the price moving with revenue.*

- **Fundamental — the dominant bucket this print, and it moved both ways.** The deep-dive's largest named fundamental catalyst, *"First rent recognition… switches on the AWS guarantee and the Google backstop,"* **half-fired, early and favourably**: Black Pearl rent commenced in August. Its named fundamental *de-rate*, *"Cash interest begins (May/Aug 2026 coupons)"* (−moderate, high confidence), **also fired exactly on schedule** — $0 → $63.9M of cash interest, OCF $335M worse sequentially. And *"First HPC segment disclosure — with Q3'26 results"* is now confirmed as coming: the 10-Q states an additional segment is expected when leases commence, which they now have.
- **Structural — unchanged in substance, worse in timing.** ERCOT Batch Zero was the report's second-largest structural de-rate (*"converts queue position from a scarce private asset into an administratively allocated one"*). It has not been resolved; it has been **suspended with no new date**, which converts a dated binary into an open-ended overhang. **A driver pushed out indefinitely is de-rated even when intact**, and this one gates the ~4.4 GW pipeline, the 900 MW Apollo option and the 200 MW Stingray expansion — i.e. everything that is *not* already contracted. - **Macro — untouched.** Bitcoin was +1.3% across the window and is now a minority factor by the report's own analysis; nothing in the print bears on the rate path or Texas power policy beyond the ERCOT item above.

**Which bucket the call weighted most: fundamental, and it netted to a de-rate.** EV/contracted NOI fell ~14.5× → ~11.4× on a denominator that rose. The market paid less for the same contracted book — and it did so in the same week the first rent on that book actually started flowing. **That gap between the multiple and the milestone is the quarter's central puzzle**, and the most defensible explanation is that the market repriced *growth* (no fourth lease, below-market contracted rents, an indefinitely-suspended ERCOT queue) rather than *conversion*.

#### (c) Risks

- **Escalated — dilution mechanics.** The fully-loaded diluted count is now **~619M against 414M outstanding**, a 49% overhang and **2.4× a year ago**. $129.2M of ATM was executed in Q2 (5,496,993 shares at an average net **$23.50**), the 10-Q names ATM sales as part of the 12-month funding plan, remaining ATM capacity is **not disclosed**, and 777,158 shares appeared between 6/30 and the 8/3 cover date **unattributed in the filing**. **Pre-Mortem update — the mechanism is unchanged and it moved closer, by a different route than the one named.** The deep-dive's single most likely path to permanent impairment is a **timing cascade**: a delay or exclusion arrives *unprotected* because guarantees are inoperative; the equity falls on the news; the Barber Lake warrant window then opens below ~$17.79, forcing issuance or cash; Cipher funds the remainder from an ATM into weakness. **This print moved the first limb further away and the middle limbs much closer.** Black Pearl commenced early, so the construction-slip trigger is weaker than it was — but the equity fell 25% to within **2.4%** of the ratchet threshold anyway, on an *earnings miss* rather than a delay. **The cascade does not require the trigger the report named.** Any 2.4% adverse move in the next eight weeks reaches the same place. The mechanism is live and its trigger is now generic.
