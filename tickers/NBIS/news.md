## Thesis context (one-paragraph)
Nebius is a full-stack AI **neocloud**: it buys NVIDIA systems, secures power and shell, and sells AI training and inference capacity with its own orchestration and inference software on top, across Finland, Estonia, the UK, France, Iceland, Israel and a fast-growing US footprint. Growth is not in question — **Q2 2026 revenue was $582.3M, +454% YoY, more in one quarter than the $529.8M of all FY2025**, at a 41% group adjusted-EBITDA margin (49.7% AI cloud). The counterparty book is the best in the cohort: **Microsoft (AAA), up to ~$17.4–19.4B through 2031** with all tranches delivered, and **Meta (Aa3)** — but the headline "$27B" is **$12B firm plus up to $15B contingent on Nebius failing to sell the capacity elsewhere**, a residual backstop, not demand. **RPO is $37,490.6M at 2026-06-30** (unaudited; the last audited figure is $21,333M in the FY2025 20-F, and the next audited check is ~April 2027), with **$5,975.2M of deferred revenue already collected** and management guiding to **">$9B of customer prepayments in 2026"** — which is why **H1 operating cash flow of +$4,504.1M is overwhelmingly prepayment, not earnings**. Against that: **H1 capex was $8,130.3M**, and **reported H1 D&A of $471.7M is ~46% of the ~$1,026M straight-line charge** on a gross PP&E base that went ~$6,190M → ~$14,320M in six months at the company's own (2026-extended, 4→5 year, unquantified) useful life — more than half the asset base is **not yet in service**. FY2025 asset turn was **0.146x against the ~0.49x GAAP breakeven requires — a 3.3x improvement**. Concentration is near-total and partly unassessable: **top three customers = 59% of Q2 revenue (24/21/14)**, none ever named, the letters **inconsistent between the 20-F and the Q2 6-K** for the same receivable, and **Customer E carries 29% of net AR on 14% of revenue** with no investment-grade description anywhere. Capital structure: ~$14.3B of face debt against ~$14.5B pro-forma cash (roughly net-cash-neutral, but H1 investing outflow was $8.29B — about two quarters of runway at that rate) after four convertible issues in fifteen months; the August 2026 deal placed **0.50% four-year and 4.50% eight-year paper**, and the issuer is **unrated**, so the early-warning signal is a **failed or repriced deal, not a downgrade**. **NVIDIA holds 9.3% via a pre-funded warrant whose lockup expired 2026-09-11, with no 13G/A since.** Founder **Arkady Volozh controls 51.62% of votes on 11.33% of the economics** via 10:1 Class B; **material weaknesses in ICFR** were identified at 2025-12-31. The stock settled at **$237.35 (2026-09-29)**, −17.2% from its $286.69 closing high — the all-time intraday high of $299.86 printed on **2026-06-22, the exact Nasdaq-100 inclusion date**, and has never been revisited — at **~20–23x FY2026E revenue** against CoreWeave's ~12.3x trailing on 5.6x the revenue. The debate is not demand. It is whether $25B of GPUs bought at scarcity prices will still be earning when they are fully depreciated.

## Edge (variant view — what the crowd may have wrong)
*Seeded **2026-09-30** from [`reports/2026-09-29.md`](reports/2026-09-29.md) §18, verbatim. First coverage — nothing superseded. Because there was no pre-committed Edge to deny, the mandatory §L adversarial pass was aimed at the **prevailing bull case** rather than at an existing thesis, and carried the fact-check duty over every load-bearing figure; it killed the insider-selling angle outright and independently **confirmed** the backlog-credibility comparison against Nscale.*

> The consensus argument for owning NBIS is hypergrowth plus counterparty quality: 454% revenue growth, Microsoft and Meta prepaying, a 41% adjusted-EBITDA margin. Every element is true and primary-sourced. **What the consensus does not price is that the metric carrying the argument is structurally incapable of showing the thing that matters.** Adjusted EBITDA excludes D&A, and **D&A is currently running at 46% of the straight-line charge** on the company's own five-year life because more than half the asset base is not in service. Strip further: **share-based compensation is ~49% of that adjusted EBITDA.** The margin the bull case quotes is calculated before the two largest costs of being this company. The forward arithmetic is the edge: at FY2025's asset turn of 0.146x and a 41% margin, Nebius generates **$0.060 of EBITDA per dollar of gross PP&E against a $0.200 depreciation charge — EBITDA is 30% of depreciation** — and GAAP breakeven requires asset turn of roughly **0.49x, a 3.3x improvement**. At the current $5.7B/quarter capex pace, gross PP&E passes $25B, and fully in service that is **~$5B/year of depreciation against $3.0B of ARR**.
>
> **So the differentiated call is this: the bull and bear cases are not two scenarios — they are the same event observed at two different times, and the market is pricing only the first half of it.** The quarter that proves the model works is the same quarter that starts the depreciation clock, and there is no path to learning one without the other. A holder who waits for "profitability" will watch adjusted EBITDA look excellent while GAAP losses widen, because the asset base entering service raises the numerator and the denominator together. **The variable to underwrite is asset turn and connected megawatts. It is not the margin, not ARR, and not the backlog.** Layer on a second mechanism nobody is watching: **operating cash flow is prepayment, not earnings.** Deferred revenue went $1,577.5M → $5,975.2M in six months against ">$9B of 2026 prepayments" guided. Prepayments are cash-positive on receipt and cash-neutral on recognition — so **OCF converges toward EBITDA the moment the prepayment book stops growing, and deceleration in new signings appears in cash before it appears in revenue.** That inverts the usual order in which an equity telegraphs trouble.
>
> **What this Edge explicitly does not claim.** It does not claim demand fails — hyperscaler capex is ~$720–745B and rising. It does not claim a customer defaults — Microsoft and Meta are among the best credits on earth. It does not claim fraud or aggressive accounting; Nebius at five years is **less** aggressive than CoreWeave at six, and its RPO is audited where the cohort's is marketed (Nscale's S-1 markets ~$103.4B of TCV on $140.6M of H1 revenue — ~368x — with only $2.6B active, against Nebius's ~12.5x and CoreWeave's ~10x). And it does not claim the business cannot work. **It claims the market is underwriting a return on capital that the reported metrics cannot yet evidence, in a window where the evidence is about to arrive either way.**

## Tripwires (pre-committed exit / re-underwrite triggers)
*Seeded 2026-09-30 from [`reports/2026-09-29.md`](reports/2026-09-29.md) §18 — verbatim, split one bullet per trigger (a formatting change only). Trigger identity (the numbering) originates here. Expiry dates in the table below (§J.4).*

Pre-commit to re-underwriting or exiting if ANY of these fire:

- **(1)** **operating cash flow decelerating in any quarter while revenue accelerates.** That single divergence is the earliest observable that the prepayment engine is slowing, and because of the inverted signal order it precedes every other symptom by a quarter or more. **Decide before the print, not after.** *(The §8 pre-mortem's leading indicator, and what kills the thesis fastest.)*
- **(2)** any disclosed **service credit, tranche termination or delivery-milestone remedy** under the Microsoft or Meta agreements. **Both contracts carry them; neither counterparty needs to default for the revenue to be reduced** — and a remedy event would strike revenue *and* the collateral of the ~$775M senior secured facility simultaneously, since that facility is secured on the contracted cash flows of "an investment-grade counterparty." *A slower trigger than #1, with its first real test at Meta's $12B Vera Rubin deployment in H1 2027 — hardware Nebius has only just sampled.*
- **(3)** **[UPWARD — re-underwrite, do not exit]** if **two consecutive quarterly prints show revenue growth with operating cash flow growing at least as fast, *and* D&A rising toward the straight-line rate without adjusted-EBITDA margin compressing below ~35%**, the capital-return concern is **falsified on its own terms** — that combination can only happen if the asset base is entering service *and* filling *at* price. **Committed action: re-underwrite upward.** *(Recorded as a numbered trigger deliberately: §18 states it as a committed action, and a falsifier that is never written down is never checked.)*

Any one = the thesis is impaired — or, for #3, vindicated; decide the action now, not after the drawdown.
