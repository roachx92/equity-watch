**Running tally: six revenue guides issued and six beaten — 6 for 6, with no cut and no withdrawal anywhere in the record.** That is a genuinely strong headline, and it is the single most important context for every forward number below. **But the table's real content is the trend inside the wins, and it runs the other way: the beat has collapsed.** Revenue came in +12.5%, +6.2%, +17.4%, +14.0% and +19.7% above the guide midpoint in the five quarters to Q3 FY26 — and then **+1.6% in Q4 FY26.** Credo has spent six quarters conditioning the market to treat its guide as a floor with a low-double-digit cushion on top; in Q4 that cushion essentially disappeared. Separately, **GAAP opex has missed its own guide to the high side in four of the last five quarters** — the least-watched and most consistent failure in the record. Against that, gross margin was sandbagged at a 63–66% guided band and beaten by 200–270bp for four straight quarters, **and that sandbag was retired at this print** (Q1 FY27 guided to 66.9–68.9% GAAP). Read together: management's guidance convention changed on two axes at once in the same quarter, and the direction of both changes removes cushion rather than adding it.

## Q4 & FY2026 — reported 2026-06-01

> **No Tripwire fired. Tripwire #1 is on early-warning, and it resolves within hours of this file being written.** A clean beat on every headline — revenue $437.0m, GAAP gross margin 68.2%, non-GAAP EPS $1.16 against a $1.03 consensus — that on inspection is the quarter where **three separate cushions came off at once**: the revenue guide-beat collapsed from +14–20% to **+1.6%**; the 63–65% long-term gross-margin model was **dropped without comment**; and management **raised** the FY2027 growth guide from ">50%" to ">80%" while simultaneously guiding H1 to mid-single-digit sequential growth — loading roughly **$1.44bn of H2 revenue** behind an optical ramp that had not started. Sequential growth went +51.9% (Q3) → **+7.4%** (Q4) → **+7.6% guided** (Q1 FY27). Meanwhile inventory rose 20.6% QoQ against 7.4% revenue growth, finished goods +189% for the year, and DIO hit a series high of 164 days. The tape's verdict was the sharpest signal of all: on 2026-06-02 the AI-optics complex re-rated violently on an NVIDIA endorsement of **Marvell**, and Credo — one day after guiding a $600m+ optical business — was paid **+1.28%** against a peer basket up ~14.6%. Net: **thesis unchanged in structure, materially tighter in execution risk; the Edge corroborated.**

So the contaminating catalyst was **a sector-specific NVIDIA endorsement of Credo's most direct optical-DSP rival** — and on the single day the AI-interconnect narrative got its largest endorsement of the year, one day after Credo guided a $600m+ optical business, the market paid Credo **+1.28%** while paying MRVL +32.5%, COHR +17.6%, FN +12.7% and ALAB +11.1%.

**The cable primes are the control group, and they settle the interpretation.** APH +1.41% and TEL +1.72% barely moved: 06-02 was an *optics/silicon-photonics* re-rating, not a copper one. **CRDO traded with the copper names, not the optics names — on the day after it asked to be reclassified as an optics company.** The 06-03 follow-through (−6.29% while SOXX +1.76%) removes the "it just needed a day" defence.

**Was it a re-rate? Yes — a de-rate, and it does not need a multiple to see.** Sell-side FY27 numbers and targets were raised materially into a flat-to-down price: Mizuho $260→$290, Craig-Hallum $200→$300, Needham $220→$275, Jefferies $225→$270 *(aggregator-sourced — indicative, not primary; see caveats)*. **Estimates up, price down ⇒ the multiple compressed.** That is the opposite of a re-rate. *Gaps named: no beta figure and no print-window short-interest figure could be sourced; forward P/E before/after is deliberately not printed rather than fabricated.*

### 2. Assessment: what the call does to the thesis

#### (a) The underlying investment thesis

The deep-dive's conjunction — **all of** (a) hyperscaler AI capex keeps compounding through FY27–28; (b) the AEC reach window converts passive-DAC ports to AEC ports faster than 224G shrinks the band and faster than racks go cable-free; (c) Credo converts a copper franchise into an optics-and-SerDes franchise before ~2028, delivering **>$600m of FY27 optical**; (d) no hyperscaler pairs a cable prime with a rival retimer die and commoditises the 68% margin.

- **(a) capex — supported, strongly.** Amphenol's IT datacom +99% with a 1.24 book-to-bill is independent, primary corroboration from the largest interconnect company on earth. Untouched by this print in any negative direction.
- **(b) the reach window — weakened, and by management's own testimony.** Brennan said 200G/lane will be *"relatively light"* in FY27 on industry-wide deployment delays. **The 224G transition is the deep-dive's single strongest structural bull mechanism, and it just slipped a year.** The FY27 case therefore rests on optical conversion, not on the durable copper mechanism. Management also conceded optical could reach 50/50 with copper and perhaps exceed it.
- **(c) optical conversion — supported in commitment, weakened in verifiability.** The number was *raised* (>$500m → >$600m), decomposed into three >$100m legs, and backed with ~$750m of cash out the door. That is management voting with capital, and it is the strongest possible form of commitment. **But every independent means of tracking it was removed or absent in the same quarter**: no product-line split in the filings, no ZeroFlap customer count, no stated basis for the 20% raise, and the whole thing 2H-weighted so verification defers to ~March 2027 — precisely Tripwire #4's test date.
- **(d) commoditisation — left untested.** Zero competitor discussion on the call, and no dated design-win at a ≥19% customer could be confirmed either way.

**Discounting the forward numbers by the Guidance track record (§ table above):** management is **6 for 6** on revenue guides, which earns real credit and argues against reflexively discounting the >80%. **But the same table shows the beat cushion collapsing to +1.6% and GAAP opex missing four of five quarters** — so the appropriate discount is not "they always beat," it is "they have never missed a *revenue* guide, they routinely miss *opex*, and the margin of safety in the revenue guide just went to roughly zero." The >80% FY27 figure has no track record at all: it is one quarter old, replaced a >50% figure, and requires **H2 ≈ $1.44bn (+49% on H1) via two consecutive ~+28% sequential quarters** to a ~$808m Q4 FY27 exit. That is not a guide with cushion; it is a guide with a hinge.

**Net: the thesis is unchanged in structure and materially tighter in execution risk.** Nothing broke. But the leg the deep-dive called durable (224G port conversion) was deferred by management, and the entire FY27 case shifted onto the leg the deep-dive called least verifiable — while the disclosure that would verify it was withdrawn.

#### (b) Re-rating drivers

- **Fundamental — mixed, net negative.** The "Q1 FY27 print beats and the FY27 exit-rate is credible" driver did not resolve; it was *loaded*. The pull-forward discount was neither removed nor confirmed — but the guide-beat cushion that would have removed it painlessly is gone. The inventory de-rate driver the deep-dive named (+20.6% QoQ vs +7.4% revenue, DIO 164 vs 148 days) **got worse, exactly as specified.** Offsetting: gross margin held at 68.2% and the *guide* moved up ~300bp, which is a genuine, durable positive on the highest-quality line in the model.
- **Structural — timing moved, and that is a de-rate on its own.** Management deferred 200G/lane to "relatively light in FY27." A structural driver pushed a year out is materially de-rated even when intact. Against that, DustPhotonics converts CPO from a threat into a product line with FY28 revenue — a genuine structural improvement in optionality, on a clock beyond the current multiple's horizon.
- **Sentiment/technical — this is where the print actually landed, and it is the dominant bucket.** The market re-rated the AI-optics complex +12–32% on an NVIDIA endorsement of Marvell and left Credo with the cable primes. The deep-dive's "extreme factor beta" and "crowded-proxy" drivers behaved exactly as described — except the proxy relationship **broke in the unhelpful direction**: Credo participated in the complex's downside (08-18, −13% on no news) but not in this upside. **The market is not yet willing to price Credo as an optics company**, which is precisely the re-rate the FY27 optical guide is asking for.
- **Macro — untouched.** No new information.

**Bucket the print weighted most: sentiment/technical, negative** — a genuine multiple compression (estimates up, price down) driven by the market's refusal to reclassify the name. The fundamental bucket was *deferred* rather than moved, which is why tonight's print carries so much of the load.

#### (c) Risks

- **Concentration — quantified and, on the honest basis, unchanged.** Q4 had four ≥10% customers (34/27/16/10) against Q3's three (39/32/17), and management guided to "increasing diversification." But the FY26 10-K's end-customer table is **D 33% / B 32% / E 19% = 84%**, top 10 ≈ 90%, and **three names = 92% of receivables** with Customer A alone at 53% (~$124m). Diversification by *addition* is real in dollars; the correlated-factor exposure is not reduced.
- **The demand/supply asymmetry — escalated and now precisely sized.** $31.9m of customer RPO (2.4% of FY26 revenue, no minimum purchase commitments anywhere) against **$346.1m of Credo's own non-cancellable FY27 purchase obligations** — a ~10.5:1 mismatch — sitting on $250.8m of inventory whose finished-goods component tripled. This is the balance-sheet form of the pull-forward question.
- **Inventory — escalated, as the deep-dive predicted.** DIO 164.3d (series high), finished goods +189%, raw materials +405%, E&O write-downs +90% to $15.1m, and it is the auditor's Critical Audit Matter.
- **Disclosure quality — escalated, and this is the risk that compounds.** Five voluntary disclosures narrowed or vanished in one quarter, all of them on the axis (optical conversion, gross-margin floor) where the thesis now carries its weight. Each removal makes the next quarter harder to audit from outside.
- **De-escalated: gross margin and solvency.** The 65% Tripwire line moved from "the standing guide floor sits below it" to "the guide floor is 190bp above it." Zero debt, $1.44bn cash pre-DustPhotonics, real FCF.

**Pre-Mortem update.** The named mechanism — *the FQ3 spike proves a pull-forward; FY27 lands materially below the guided >80% because optical under-delivers against Marvell's 60–70% and Broadcom's >30% share; a licensed cable prime qualifies a competing AEC at one of the three customers that are 84% of revenue; gross margin compresses from 68% toward the 50s; and a 37.5× forward multiple re-rates on a 3.23-beta stock with no short base* — moved **closer on two arms and no closer on two**. Closer: the pull-forward arm (beat cushion gone, 224G deferred, inventory building) and the optical-under-delivery arm (unverifiable, 2H-weighted, tracking KPI withdrawn). Not closer: the margin-compression arm (guided *up* ~300bp) and the cable-prime arm (nothing confirmable). **The near arm is now unambiguously the FY27 revenue-shape arm, and it begins resolving tonight.**

### 3. Final thoughts — what to watch going forward

**1. The read in one line.** Credo delivered a clean quarter and a bigger promise, and in the same breath removed most of the evidence anyone could use to check the promise — the beat cushion, the margin floor, the ZeroFlap customer count and the reason the optical number went up 20% all disappeared at once, leaving a thesis that now rests almost entirely on an H2 FY27 ramp nobody outside the company can track.

**2. The directional lean.** 🔴 **cautious next ~12 months · 🟢 constructive over 3–5 years** — the near-term is a single un-cushioned binary against a guide with no margin of safety and a market that just declined to reclassify the name, while the long-dated position (68% gross margin, $1.44bn pre-deal cash, zero debt, real SerDes IP, and CPO converted from threat to product line) got structurally better. *A lean on the print, not a rating on the stock — **not financial advice**.*

**3. ✅ What to watch — priority-ordered.**
- ⚠️ **#1 — the Q1 FY27 print, TONIGHT (2026-09-01, after the close).** **Pass:** revenue ≥$465m *and* the >80% FY27 growth guide reaffirmed *and* a 2H shape that keeps H2 ≈ $1.44bn arithmetic. **Fail:** below **$465m**, or the >80% is withdrawn/lowered/not reaffirmed, or no 2H shape → **Tripwire #1 fires → pre-committed action is exit/re-underwrite.** Distance from threshold: the guide floor *is* the threshold, and the historical +14–20% cushion collapsed to +1.6% last quarter. **This single date resolves more than everything else on this list combined.**
- **Q2 FY27 guide, given tonight — the under-watched half of the same test.** Management said to expect "something similar" to Q1's mid-single-digit sequential. **Pass:** ≥~$493m keeps H1 on the arithmetic. **Fail:** a Q2 guide below ~$490m makes the >80% require an H2 step-change no company delivers, and Tripwire #1's third limb ("declines to give a 2H shape that makes it arithmetic") becomes live even if Q1 itself printed fine.
- **Optical revenue disclosure — open, no date, and this is the structural one.** Does Credo restore any product-line split, or give a ZeroFlap customer count? **Pass:** a filed or stated optical number that can be tracked. **Fail:** continued silence → the >$600m guide stays unauditable to **Tripwire #4's ~March 2027 test**, by which point a miss is a print, not a warning.
- **Inventory and DIO direction — next print, tonight.** **Pass:** DIO retreats from 164 days as the guided ramp consumes finished goods. **Fail:** DIO extends again with finished goods still growing faster than revenue → the pre-build was demand that isn't coming, and E&O charges (already +90%) tell you first.
- **Q1 FY27 cash after DustPhotonics — tonight.** ~$750m left the balance sheet in this quarter. **Watch:** cash landing near ~$650–850m with OCF intact confirms management's ~$200m/quarter operating cash flow framing; materially below would be the first genuine balance-sheet question this name has faced.
- **Any TE / Molex / Amphenol AEC qualification at a hyperscaler — open, before end-CY2026.** **Fail:** a named design-win at a ≥19% customer → **Tripwire #2 fires.**
- **NVIDIA architecture and ecosystem disclosures — open.** MGX adoption breadth at the **scale-out** tier specifically, and whether Credo ever appears in an NVLink Fusion-style partner list. **Fail:** scale-out AECs designed out, or a named rival included while Credo is excluded → **Tripwire #5 fires.**

**4. 💲 Where the price sits.** At the 2026-06-03 close of $214.60 the stock had given back the print and then some, into a sell-side that had just raised targets to $270–300 — a **multiple compression, not a price move**: estimates went up and the price went down. Since then the name has traded $206–283 and closed 2026-08-31 at **$226.19**, which is *below* the pre-print 2026-05-29 close of $236.03. Three months and a raised FY27 guide have produced a lower price. The market has been unwilling to pay for the optical story on management's word alone — it is waiting for the print, which is the correct posture and also the reason the print carries so much.

**5. 🔀 What would flip the read.**
- 🟢 **Turns bullish:** a Q1 FY27 print **meaningfully above $475m** with the >80% reaffirmed *and* a Q2 guide that keeps H1 ≈ $963m — that restores the cushion, converts the FY27 shape from a hinge into a ramp, and forces the reclassification the 06-02 tape refused. A restored optical revenue disclosure would independently do most of the same work.
- 🔴 **Confirms bearish:** revenue **below $465m**, or the **>80% withdrawn, lowered or left unreaffirmed**, or a Q2 guide that makes H2 arithmetically implausible — **any one fires Tripwire #1**. Second-order: **DIO extending past 164 days** with finished goods still building.

**6. Why the lean.**
- 🔴 **(12mo) The margin of safety was spent, not earned.** Six straight beats of +6% to +20% over the guide midpoint, then **+1.6%**. Whatever conservatism was in Credo's revenue guide is gone, and the next guide is the one Tripwire #1 tests. A company that stops sandbagging right before its highest-stakes print has removed the buffer that made the last six prints easy.
- 🔴 **(12mo) The FY27 case migrated to the leg nobody can check, in the quarter the checks were removed.** Management deferred 200G/lane — the durable structural mechanism — to "relatively light in FY27," raised optical from $500m to $600m with no stated reason, declined to rank the three legs, withheld the ZeroFlap customer count, and reports one segment with no product-line split. **>$600m of a ~$2.4bn year now rests on a number that will never appear on a filed line item.**
- 🔴 **(12mo) The tape delivered a verdict that was not about the tape.** On the biggest AI-interconnect day of the year, Credo traded with the copper cable primes (+1.4%, +1.7%) and not with the optics complex (+9% to +32%). That is the market declining to grant the re-rate the entire FY27 optical guide is asking for — and it did so **after** hearing the guide.
- 🟢 **(3–5yr) The honest counterweight, and it is substantial.** This is a business earning **68.2% gross margin** with a **series-low 48.6-day DSO and receivables falling in absolute dollars** — the cleanest possible refutation of the channel-stuffing reading of the inventory build. It generated **$407m of FY26 free cash flow with zero debt**, is **6 for 6 on revenue guides with no cut or withdrawal ever**, moved its gross-margin guide floor *up* ~300bp, added a fourth ≥10% customer, and converted CPO from an existential threat into a product line it now sells into. Management committed ~$750m of its own cash to the optical transition rather than talking about it. **Nothing in this print threatened the franchise; what it threatened was the timetable.**

**7. Bottom line.** Q4 FY2026 was a good quarter that quietly became a harder investment case. The company is executing — margins, cash generation and collections are all excellent, and management put three-quarters of a billion dollars behind the transition it says is coming. But it also spent its guidance cushion, deferred the copper mechanism the bull case called durable, moved the whole FY27 story onto an optical ramp that is 2H-weighted and unauditable from any filing, and stopped publishing the metric that tracked it. The market's response — targets up, price down, and Credo left behind by the very complex it wants to join — reads that fairly. **Everything now runs through one date, and it is tonight: $465m is the floor, the >80% is the reaffirmation, and the Q2 guide is the tell.** *Not financial advice — informational research tooling only.*
